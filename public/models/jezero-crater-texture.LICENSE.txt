jezero-crater-texture.jpg is the base color texture of the 3D model "Jezero Crater (Mars)",
flipped north-up and re-encoded as JPEG. Only the texture is used; the app's terrain is the
USGS CTX DTM.

the texture covers lon 77.0095..78.5345 E, lat 17.5661..19.3019 N (GCS Mars 2000), north up.
